## 🛠️ Installation for Buyers

### After Purchase
1. Download the resource from Tebex
2. Extract to your server's `resources` folder
3. Read the installation guide: `docs/installation.md`
4. Configure `config.lua` to your preferences
5. Add to `server.cfg`: `ensure lxr-houserob`
6. Restart your server

### Dependencies (Required)
- RSG-Core (or compatible framework)
- ox_lib
- ox_target (optional)
- jo_libs
